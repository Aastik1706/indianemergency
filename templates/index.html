EXECUTIVE BRIEFING

How the Pune Emergency AI Operates

A plain-English explanation of the end-to-end data pipeline, machine learning modeling, and decision-support architecture.

1

Distress Call Received

A distress call reaches the PMC Fire Control Room (e.g., a commercial fire in Bhavani Peth or vehicle collision on Nagar Road). The dispatcher logs the origin station and incident coordinates.

2

Real-Time Friction Evaluated

The system assesses current friction: distance, 18:30 evening gridlock, heavy monsoon downpours (>15 mm/hr), and narrow 3-meter heritage alleyways in Pune's central peth areas.

3

Gradient Boosting Inference

Our champion Gradient Boosting model evaluates 15 engineered features, predicting transit time with a tight Mean Absolute Error of 0.83 minutes and calculating a 90% confidence window.

4

Proactive Early Delay Warning

If arrival is forecasted to exceed the 20-minute municipal threshold, the system triggers an operational alert, prompting dispatchers to coordinate with traffic police for green corridor clearance.

Frequently Addressed Questions

Why not simply use consumer navigation apps like Google Maps?
Consumer navigation APIs calculate speed for passenger sedans. Heavy multi-ton fire tenders cannot maneuver narrow 3-meter peth alleys or navigate waterlogged underpasses at passenger speeds. Emergency vehicles also possess siren clearance rights on major bypasses. Our model captures these unique operational physics.
Does this system replace municipal dispatchers?
No. This is strictly a decision-support and city-planning platform. It equips human dispatchers with quantitative foresight during severe weather and helps municipal authorities determine optimal locations for future fire sub-stations.
Why is the project focused specifically on Pune?
Urban transit dynamics, rainfall intensity, and heritage road networks vary drastically across Indian cities. A generalized "pan-India" model without localized calibration would lack predictive accuracy. Grounding the study in Pune's 14 stations and 15 wards ensures empirical validity.
